### **1.2 WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?**

* **Automatically collected information:** Information such as your IP address and device characteristics may be collected automatically via third-party SDKs (e.g., Google Firebase). This is required for the technical operation and security of the app. We also receive basic, anonymized crash reports via the standard Google Play Console to improve app stability.
* **In-app purchases & payment data:** The application offers premium content via in-app purchases. All transactions are fully handled and stored via the **Google Play Billing** system. We do not process or store any of your financial information (e.g., credit card numbers).
* **Game Statistics (Firebase):** We use Google Firebase Firestore to collect completely anonymous, global usage statistics (e.g., how often a game mode is started worldwide). No personal data or unique user identifiers are linked to these statistics.
* **Local Game Data:** All game settings, purchased deck states, and local histories are stored exclusively on your local device.

---

### **1.3 HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION? (Legal Basis & Retention)**
We process your personal information based on the GDPR:
* **Contractual Necessity (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR):** To facilitate in-app purchases made through Google Play.
* **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR):** To keep our Services safe, functional, and to analyze anonymous global usage trends.

**Retention:** We only retain data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

---

### **1.4 DATA TRANSFERS & THIRD PARTIES**
We share data with the following providers:
* **Payment Processors:** [Google Play Billing](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-aup)
* **Backend Infrastructure:** [Google Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

### **1.5 YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS & DATA DELETION**
Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. 

Since this application stores all game progress locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app's data in your Android Device Settings (`Settings > Apps > Who Pays > Storage > Clear Data`) or by uninstalling the app.

If you have contacted us via email and wish for your communication history to be deleted, please send a request to **rotti.dev@gmail.com**.
