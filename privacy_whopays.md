### **1.2 WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?**

* **Automatically collected information & Advertising:** Information such as your IP address, device characteristics, and the Android Advertising ID (AAID) is collected automatically via third-party SDKs. This is required for the technical operation and to serve advertisements via **Google AdMob**.
* **In-app purchases & payment data:** The application offers premium content. All transactions are fully handled via **Google Play Billing**. We do not process or store any of your financial information.
* **Game Statistics (Firebase):** We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous, global usage statistics (e.g., how often a game mode is played). No unique user identifiers are linked to this.
* **Play Games Services:** We may use Google Play Games Services for achievements or cloud saving. If used, your progress is saved securely via your Google account. We have no direct access to your private Google Drive files.

---

### **1.3 HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?**
We process your personal information based on the GDPR:
* **Consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR):** For personalized advertising via **Google AdMob**. You grant this consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) within the app.
* **Contractual Necessity (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR):** To facilitate in-app purchases made through Google Play.
* **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR):** To keep our Services safe, functional, and to analyze anonymous global trends.

---

### **1.4 DATA TRANSFERS & THIRD PARTIES**
We share data with the following providers (data may be transferred outside the EU/EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses):
* **Ad Networks:** [Google AdMob & Ads Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Payment Processors:** [Google Play Billing](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-aup)
* **Backend Infrastructure:** [Google Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

### **1.5 YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS & DATA DELETION**
Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. 

* **Local Data:** You can delete local game progress by clearing the app's data in your Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > Who Pays > Storage > Clear Data`).
* **Advertising (Opt-Out):** You can withdraw your consent for personalized ads within the app's settings or by resetting your device's Advertising ID (`Settings > Google > Ads`).
* **Cloud Data:** To delete Play Games data, go to the Google Play Games app (`Settings > Delete Play Games account & data`).

For communication history deletion requests, please contact **rotti.dev@gmail.com**.
